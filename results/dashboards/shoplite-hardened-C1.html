What was found

DELETE /orders/{order_id} (order-service) is deprecated but got 6 calls

The spec marks the operation deprecated, but clients still call it, so it cannot be removed and keeps receiving less attention.

Evidence (high confidence)

  • spec: deprecated: true
  • 6 calls observed

Sources: callchain, openapi, runtime_traffic

Why score 9 (medium)

  • likelihood base 2
  • impact base 3
  • likelihood +1: reachable from the internet

Leads to: Sensitive data exposure. Best attack path APCR 9.

Recommendation

Migrate callers off DELETE /orders/{order_id} and remove it.

  1. Find the remaining callers (traces show them), migrate them, then remove the operation.
  2. Add a sunset date and log every call until removal.

What was found

2 trace(s) start at user-service GET /internal/users/export with no traced caller

Traces start at this backend endpoint, so the caller is not instrumented and did not come through the gateway. Monitoring cannot say who made these calls.

Evidence (high confidence)

  • 2 root spans at user-service
  • no parent span from the gateway or another service

Sources: callchain

Why score 9 (medium)

  • likelihood base 3
  • impact base 3

Leads to: Undetected attacker activity. Best attack path APCR 4.5.

Recommendation

Identify and instrument the caller of GET /internal/users/export.

  1. Instrument every job and client that calls internal APIs (propagate traceparent).
  2. Require caller identity on internal endpoints so logs name the caller.
